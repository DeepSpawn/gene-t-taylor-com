## Work

Leading one of two teams on Jira's Issue Backend. We're extracting the core issue API from the Jira monolith into the Issue Service, which uses a DynamoDB document model in place of the existing normalized SQL representation. My team owns the migration path; our sibling team owns the new service. The hard part right now is moving traffic onto it without disrupting reads and writes that have been hitting the monolith for over a decade.

Doing my best to keep current with industry trends and drive effective LLM-augmented development practices across the team and department.
